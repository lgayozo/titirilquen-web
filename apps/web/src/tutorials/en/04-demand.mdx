# 3. Demand

Each agent evaluates the utility of every available mode and chooses via a **multinomial logit**:

$$
P(i) = \frac{e^{V_i}}{\sum_{j} e^{V_j}}
$$

<OverleafRef sec="Demanda · Elección de modo" />

## Utilities per mode

**Car** — the cost has a fixed component (parking) and a **per-kilometre** one (fuel):

$$
V_{\text{auto}}^s = ASC_{\text{auto}}^s + \beta_{t_{\text{viaje}}}^s \cdot t_{\text{viaje}} + \beta_c^s \cdot \left(c_{\text{parking}} + c_{\text{combustible}} \cdot d_i\right)
$$

<OverleafRef sec="Modelo de Demanda" tag="R-14" />

with $d_i$ the distance (km) from origin to the CBD.

**Metro** — three time components, each with its own sensitivity:

$$
V_{\text{metro}}^s = ASC_{\text{metro}}^s + \beta_{t_{\text{viaje}}}^s \cdot t_{\text{viaje}} + \beta_{t_{\text{espera}}}^s \cdot t_{\text{espera}} + \beta_{t_{\text{caminata}}}^s \cdot t_{\text{caminata}} + \beta_c^s \cdot c_{\text{tarifa}}
$$

<OverleafRef sec="Modelo de Demanda" />

where $t_{\text{caminata}}$ is the **station access time** ($t_a$ in the train-supply notation) and $t_{\text{espera}}$ the platform wait ($t_e$).

**Bicycle** — **additive stepped** physical penalties and a feasibility cutoff:

$$
V^s_{\text{bicicleta}} = \begin{cases}
-\infty & \text{if } t_{\text{bici}} > 45 \\
ASC_{\text{bici}}^s + \beta_{t_{\text{viaje}}}^s \cdot t_{\text{bici}} + \displaystyle\sum_{k \in \{10, 20, 30\}} \mathbb{1}\{t_{\text{bici}} > k\} \cdot \pi_k^s & \text{if } t_{\text{bici}} \le 45
\end{cases}
$$

<OverleafRef sec="Modelo de Demanda" tag="R-2" />

**Walking** — with its **own sensitivity** $\beta_{t_{\text{caminata}}}^s$ (not $\beta_{t_{\text{viaje}}}^s$), thresholds $\{5, 15, 25\}$ and a 30-min cutoff:

$$
V^s_{\text{caminata}} = \begin{cases}
-\infty & \text{if } t_{\text{caminata}} > 30 \\
ASC_{\text{caminata}}^s + \beta_{t_{\text{caminata}}}^s \cdot t_{\text{caminata}} + \displaystyle\sum_{k \in \{5, 15, 25\}} \mathbb{1}\{t_{\text{caminata}} > k\} \cdot \pi_k^s & \text{if } t_{\text{caminata}} \le 30
\end{cases}
$$

<OverleafRef sec="Modelo de Demanda" tag="R-3" />

<Callout type="info">
Penalties $\pi_k^s$ are **additive constants** added when each fatigue threshold is exceeded — not coefficients multiplying time. The penalty thresholds (10/20/30 bike, 5/15/25 walk) are a different concept from the **feasibility cutoffs** (45 and 30 min), where the mode leaves the choice set.
</Callout>

## Pedagogical breakdown

The **Transport** page includes a "Utility inspector" showing the decomposition:
- ASC component (constant per mode/stratum)
- $\beta_t \cdot T$ component (time sensitivity)
- $\beta_c \cdot C$ component (cost sensitivity)
- Physical penalties component (bike/walk)

It lets the student see **why** a mode wins in each context.
