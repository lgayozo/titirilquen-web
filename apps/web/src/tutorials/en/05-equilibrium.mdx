# 4. Equilibrium

In the first iteration agents have no congestion information and assume **free flow**. When demand is assigned, the car overloads (it's the fastest). To avoid oscillations, the **Method of Successive Averages** is used:

$$
T_{\text{actuales}}^{*} = f \cdot T_{\text{nuevos}} + (1 - f) \cdot T_{\text{actuales}}, \qquad f = \frac{1}{it + 1}
$$

<OverleafRef sec="Bucle de equilibrio" />

The weight of new information decreases as $1/it$: the first iteration replaces fully, the second averages 50/50, the third weighs 1/3 and 2/3, etc.

## Interpretation

- Large $f$ in early iterations → fast adaptation (the city "learns")
- Small $f$ in late iterations → stabilization (the city "remembers")

## Stopping criterion

The loop stops when the residual

$$
r^{(it)} = \left\lVert T^{(it)} - T^{(it-1)} \right\rVert_{\infty}
$$

<OverleafRef sec="Criterio de Parada" tag="R-11" />

— the maximum travel-time change over any mode and parcel — falls below the tolerance (reference: **0.1 min**) for **two consecutive iterations** (robust to Monte Carlo noise). `MAX_ITER` (reference: 20) remains as a safety cap: the MSA's $\sim 1/it$ tail can need 20–25 iterations in stiff scenarios.

## Coupled land ↔ transport loop

The outer loop (**City at equilibrium** page) alternates both modules to the joint fixed point: land use allocates households given accessibility $T$, that population runs the MSA, and the new times update $T$. Each parcel's accessibility is the expected travel time to the CBD, **common to all strata** (population-weighted mean):

$$
T^{\text{new}}(i) = \sum_h \frac{H_h}{\sum_g H_g} \cdot \mathbb{E}\left[\, t \mid h, i \,\right]
$$

<OverleafRef sec="Acoplamiento suelo–transporte" tag="R-12" />

with outer damping $T \leftarrow \theta\, T^{\text{new}} + (1-\theta)\, T$, $\theta = 1/(n+1)$, and residual $\lVert T^{\text{new}} - T \rVert_\infty$ in **minutes** (reference tolerance: 1 min).

<Callout type="info">
Why a common $T$ and not per stratum? Accessibility is an attribute of the **location**; if it were per stratum, the lower times of motorized strata would read as "distance doesn't bother them" and would **invert** the bid-rent ordering (rich at the periphery). Valuation heterogeneity is captured by $\alpha_h$.
</Callout>
