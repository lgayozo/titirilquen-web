# 1. City

The city is abstracted as a **discretized ruler** with $n_{\text{cells}}$ parcels of uniform width $\Delta x = L / n_{\text{cells}}$.

$$
\text{position}(i) = (i + 0.5) \cdot \Delta x, \quad i = 0, 1, \ldots, n_{\text{cells}} - 1
$$

<OverleafRef sec="Creación de la ciudad" />

The **CBD** sits at $i_{\text{CBD}} = \lfloor n_{\text{cells}} / 2 \rfloor$ (central cell). All trips head to this cell.

## Population

Population is parameterized with a **physical density** $\rho_{\text{pop}}$ in inhabitants/km — not per cell — so the grid is a purely numerical choice:

$$
N_{\text{pop}} = \rho_{\text{pop}} \cdot L
$$

<OverleafRef sec="Creación de la ciudad" tag="R-8" />

The reference is $\rho_{\text{pop}} = 500$ pop/km ($\equiv$ 50 households per 100 m block). Refining the grid does **not** change population or congestion: the three dials are orthogonal — `n_cells` is resolution, *density* controls how dense the city is, and *length* how large.

Households split into 3 **socioeconomic strata** with configurable shares:

| Stratum | Share (default) | Income $y_h$ (land-use module) |
|---------|--------------:|--------------:|
| High (1) |  10 % | \$3.5M /month |
| Middle (2) |  40 % | \$1.5M /month |
| Low (3) |  50 % | \$0.5M /month |

<Callout type="info">
The income $y_h$ belongs to the **land-use module** (in \$/month; it feeds the monthly cost/income burden metric). In the transport module, income heterogeneity enters via each stratum's $\beta^s_c$, not as an explicit variable.
</Callout>

Each agent has sampled attributes:
- `teleworks`: with probability $P^s_{\text{tele}}$ (per stratum)
- `owns_car`: with probability $P^s_{\text{auto}}$

<Callout type="warn">
**Strong assumption**: every worker leaves their origin cell to the CBD and
returns — exclusive Home–Work–Home *tours*. No trip chaining.
</Callout>

## Slope

The city has a global slope $p$ (%) affecting bicycle speeds (differently for cells left or right of the CBD).
