# 6. Experimenting

## Available tools

**Transport** ([go](/sandbox)): free experimentation with the supply–demand equilibrium. Adjust any parameter and see results live.

**Land use** ([go](/land-use)): standalone Alonso equilibrium (logit auction). **City at equilibrium** ([go](/coupled)): the full land ↔ transport loop.

**Compare** ([go](/compare)): load up to 4 configurations in parallel and see deltas side by side.

## Suggested activities

### A. Parking price effect
1. In Transport, vary parking between $3k, $6k, $15k.
2. Watch car mode share change.
3. Which stratum is more sensitive? (Hint: $\beta_c$ parameter)

### B. The logit artifact
1. Under Land use (the app always runs logit), vary the high stratum's λ (0.5 → 3).
2. Watch the spatial allocation change — even though λ is just the marginal utility of income.
3. Raise λ ⇒ the stratum disperses (more noise); lower it ⇒ it concentrates. It is the scale of the choice noise (~1/βλ), not a real income effect.
4. Why was the λ dependence an inconsistency rather than a real income effect? (Hint: Land-use tutorial, correction section.)

### C. Bikeway capacity
1. Start with `cap_bike` = 800 bikes/h.
2. Raise to 5000 (*Pro-Bike* preset).
3. Where does demand migrate from?

### D. Alonso effect
1. Under Land use, set $\alpha_h$ = 12 for the high stratum and 3 for the low one (utils/min).
2. Observe the distribution. Who lives near the CBD?
3. Invert $\alpha$ values and repeat.


### E. MSA sensitivity
1. Under Transport, lower `max_iter` to 3.
2. Does it converge? What happens to the residual?
3. Raise it to 20 and watch the asymptotic behavior.

## Persistence

Every configuration can be exported as `.ttrq.json`:
- **Export**: share with peers or save for grading.
- **Import**: load teacher's scenario.
- **Share via link**: state is URL-compressed.

<Callout type="info">
**For instructors**: every activity can be shared as a link. Students open the link, run, and export their observations for submission.
</Callout>
